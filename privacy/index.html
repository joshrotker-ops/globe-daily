Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

No accounts and no sign-up. Your scores and streak stay in your own browser. We do not sell personal information.

1. What we collect

Your guesses, scores and streak are saved in your browser’s local storage so that each day’s game can be played once and your streak carries over. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data erases it.

2. The contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter, and use them only to respond. Messages are handled by our form provider, Web3Forms, which delivers them to us by email.

3. Hosting and server logs

Globe Daily is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any website, our host processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure.

4. Children

Globe Daily is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices and rights

You can clear stored game data in your browser settings, block cookies, or play in a private window. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Use the contact page to make a request.

6. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If we add advertising or analytics, this page will be updated before they go live.
